Realm Command

Gather, build, muster your forces and chart every corner of an unknown land.

Objective

Uncover the entire map. Start with a Town Hall and a handful of Peasants. Mine gold, chop lumber, raise Farms to feed your people, and train Scout Riders at the Barracks to explore quickly. Wild beasts guard the far reaches — bring Footmen and Archers, or build Watchtowers to see farther.

Field manual

Economy

Peasants mine gold from mines and chop lumber from trees, returning both to the nearest Town Hall. Every unit eats one food; Town Halls give 6 and Farms 4. Right-click a resource with Peasants selected to gather it, or set a Town Hall's rally point on a gold mine so new Peasants go to work automatically.

Buildings

Town Hall trains Peasants and receives resources. Farm feeds 4 units. Barracks trains Footmen, Archers and Scout Riders. Watchtower sees very far and shoots arrows. Select a Peasant, press B, choose a building and click a spot; more Peasants on a site build it faster.

Exploration & wilderness

The Charted bar shows how much of the map you have seen. Scout Riders are fast and see far. Wolf dens and ogre camps guard remote lands and their gold mines; slaying beasts pays a gold bounty.
